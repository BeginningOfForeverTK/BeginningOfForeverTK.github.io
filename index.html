You Are Summoned

Dear Beloved Guest,

After years of circling each other like two planets with terrible navigation skills, Tanuj and Kanchan have finally decided to make it official. Consider this your formal, legally non-binding summons to witness the moment two people voluntarily choose chaos — together, forever.

We have crossed rivers, survived families, and sat through each other's questionable music taste. If that isn't love, we don't know what is. And now, we'd like you — yes, you — to be there when we sign the paperwork that makes it someone else's problem too.

The Details (Read Carefully)

  • When: The date has been revealed on your scratch card — check the invitation! Be there, or we will assume you were snapped by Thanos and donate your seat to someone with better punctuality.
  • Where: Indraprashta Tower, Akola, Maharashtra. It's on the map — we checked. Twice. Bring the link, not excuses.
  • Dress Code: None! Wear whatever makes you feel wonderful. Come in your finest, your comfiest, or anything in between — as long as you're there, you're dressed perfectly.
  • Food: Yes, there will be food. Good food. This is the primary reason we know you'll show up, and we've made peace with that.

We have carefully curated an evening of dancing, laughter, slightly embarrassing speeches, and at least one uncle who will absolutely steal the dancefloor. The DJ has been briefed. The caterer has been warned. The chaos is pre-approved.

Please RSVP by June 10, 2026 using the RSVP section below. If you do not respond, we will assume you are permanently lost in the Upside Down and will give your slice of cake to someone we like slightly more. This is not a threat. This is a promise.

We are so glad you exist, and even more glad you're going to be there. May the day be full of joy, the night full of memories, and the morning full of excellent stories.

With all our love (and mild panic),

Tanuj & Kanchan
